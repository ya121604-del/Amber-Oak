A neighborhood roastery,
built by hand

Amber & Oak started as a single drum roaster in a garage and a stubborn belief that coffee should taste like where it came from. Today, we still roast every batch on-site, in small quantities, and taste-test each one before it reaches your cup.

Our beans travel from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Sumatra, chosen for character over convenience. No shortcuts, no syrups pretending to be flavor — just coffee, milk, and time.
